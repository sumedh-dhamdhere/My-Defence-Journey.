Frequently asked

Project FAQ

Is My Defence Journey an official defence website?

No. It is an educational student project. Official websites and current notifications should be used for final application and eligibility decisions.

Does the dashboard save my data online?

No. The current project stores study tasks, progress and demo contact submissions locally in the browser using localStorage.

Why do exam dates sometimes change?

Government and recruiting authorities can update schedules or issue notices. The project therefore displays a clear verification reminder instead of treating its own content as permanent.

Where can I verify the latest information?

Use the official UPSC, Join Indian Navy and Indian Air Force sources linked from the Defence Exams page and footer.
