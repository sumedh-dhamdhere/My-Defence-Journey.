Career awareness

Choose a Defence Career Path

Army, Navy and Air Force careers include different branches, entries and training routes. Start with broad awareness, then read the official entry rules for the specific role.

LAND • ARMY

Indian Army

The Army offers officer and other entry pathways. For student planning, common routes include NDA after 12th and CDS after graduation, alongside separate recruitment schemes and technical entries.

  • Officer-entry awareness through NDA and CDS
  • Leadership, discipline and field-oriented preparation
  • Different entries have different qualifications

SEA • NAVY

Indian Navy

The Navy includes officer and sailor/Agniveer pathways, with academic and technical options depending on the entry. Always check the specific notification for age, subjects, marks and medical standards.

  • Officer and Agniveer pathways differ
  • Technical entries may require specific subjects
  • Physical and medical standards matter
Explore Navy entries →
✈️
AIR • AIR FORCE

Indian Air Force

The Air Force has officer pathways such as AFCAT and NDA-related entries, with Flying and Ground Duty branches having different educational and age conditions.

  • AFCAT covers applicable officer branches
  • Maths and Physics are important for several technical/flying routes
  • AFSB and medical stages follow applicable entries

Career planning

Questions to ask before choosing an entry

🎯

What is my goal?

Officer, sailor or another defence-related career path can require a very different preparation plan.

🎓

What is my education?

School and graduation qualifications determine which entries you can explore.

🧠

What do I enjoy?

Consider subjects, leadership, technology, aviation, maritime work and other interests.

✅

What does the notice say?

Use the latest official notification to confirm the exact route instead of relying on general summaries.

Career choice is a process, not a guess.

Learn the broad route, compare your current qualification, prepare and then verify the precise entry conditions.
