About the project

My Defence Journey

A student-focused web project built to organise defence-career awareness into a clear, practical and easy-to-use learning experience.

✓
Educational project • information checked on 5 September 2026

Exam rules, dates and eligibility can change. This website summarizes official information for learning and directs students to the issuing authority for final verification.

What is this website?

A learning companion, not a recruitment portal

My Defence Journey brings together four connected needs of a student exploring defence careers: understanding the exams, comparing career paths, planning preparation and tracking personal study progress.

The project is intentionally designed in simple language. A student can start from the Home page, move to Defence Exams for factual basics, read Career guidance, follow the Preparation page and then use the Dashboard for personal planning.

The website does not claim to replace government recruitment portals. Its role is to help students understand what to look for and where to verify it.

🎯

Project objective

Make important defence-exam and career concepts easier for students to discover without overwhelming them with scattered information.

🧩

Project approach

Combine informational pages with small client-side tools such as a study planner, countdown, progress tracker and basic eligibility guidance.

Project mission

Learn. Prepare. Track. Verify.

The site encourages students to move from motivation to informed preparation.

What has been included

Project features

Each part of the website has a clear purpose.

🗂️

Organised information

Dedicated pages explain exams, career choices, preparation and project purpose with consistent navigation.

📊

Personal dashboard

Students can keep browser-local study tasks, progress values and an exam countdown without needing a backend.

🔎

Source-first approach

The exam page points to official UPSC, Indian Navy and Indian Air Force sources for current verification.

📱

Responsive interface

The layout adapts to desktop, tablet and mobile screens so the same project remains usable across devices.

♿

Accessible basics

Semantic sections, labels, keyboard-friendly controls and reduced-motion support improve usability.

🖱️

Defence-themed interaction

The click effect uses a subtle gold crosshair ripple to fit the site's theme while keeping the normal pointer usable.

STAGE 01

Awareness
