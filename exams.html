UPSC • Officer entry

NDA & NA

National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination for entry to training associated with the Army, Navy and Air Force through the relevant academy wings.

Education: Army Wing requires 12th-class/equivalent. Air Force and Naval Wings require 12th with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

2026-II: Notification 20 May 2026; examination 13 September 2026.

Selection: Written examination followed by SSB and subsequent medical/merit requirements.

⚓
Indian Navy • Agniveer entry

Agniveer SSR

A sailor-entry route for eligible candidates in the Indian Navy. The published SSR criteria include academic, age and physical requirements plus a staged selection process.

Education: 10+2 with Mathematics & Physics and 50% aggregate, or the specified diploma/vocational alternatives.

2026 cycles: The official Navy notice covered Agniveer SSR 01/2026 and 02/2026 batches; timelines were published as tentative.

Selection: INET Stage I, shortlisting and Stage II recruitment/physical and medical requirements as notified.

✈️
Indian Air Force • Officer entry

AFCAT

Air Force Common Admission Test for eligible candidates applying for Flying and Ground Duty branches through the relevant AFCAT notification.

Flying: 10+2 with Mathematics and Physics plus a qualifying graduation/engineering route; exact marks and branch conditions are notification-specific.

Age: AFCAT 02/2026 lists 20–24 for Flying and 20–26 for Ground Duty as on 1 July 2027.

Selection: AFCAT followed by AFSB, medical examination and merit for applicable entries.

🛡️
UPSC • Graduate officer entry

CDS

Combined Defence Services Examination for graduate candidates seeking entry to IMA, INA, Air Force Academy or OTA according to the applicable course and eligibility.

Education: Degree for IMA/OTA; specified engineering or science routes for INA; degree with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 or BE for Air Force Academy.

2026-II: Notification 20 May 2026; examination 13 September 2026.

Selection: Written examination followed by SSB and medical/merit requirements.
