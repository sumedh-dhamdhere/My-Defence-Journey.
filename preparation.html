What to build

Four strong preparation pillars

Use the exact syllabus and exam pattern from the relevant notification, then organise your daily routine around it.

📘

Academic foundation

Strengthen the subjects relevant to your exam. NDA includes mathematics and GAT-related study; CDS includes English, General Knowledge and, for IMA/INA/AFA, Elementary Mathematics.

🧪

Timed practice

Solve previous papers, topic-wise questions and timed mock tests. Review mistakes instead of only counting marks.

📰

Current affairs

Follow reliable news and revise major national, international, science, sports and defence-related developments appropriate to your exam.

🏃

Physical readiness

Build general fitness gradually and follow the specific physical and medical standards in your relevant recruitment notification.
